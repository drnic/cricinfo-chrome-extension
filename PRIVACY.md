# Cricinfo Tweaks privacy policy

Cricinfo Tweaks does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data.

## What the extension stores

The extension stores one display preference: whether commentary headlines show in ALL CAPS. It saves this preference with `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Google syncs the preference to your other devices. The extension author cannot see it.

## What the extension reads

The extension runs only on www.cricinfo.com and www.espncricinfo.com. It adds CSS to those pages to hide ads and change the style of commentary headlines. It does not read your browsing history, page content, or form data.

## Network requests

The extension makes no network requests. It contains no analytics or tracking code, and it loads no remote code.

## Contact

If you have questions, open an issue at https://github.com/drnic/cricinfo-chrome-extension/issues.
